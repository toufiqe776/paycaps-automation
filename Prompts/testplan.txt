IMPORTANT EXPLORATION RULES
===========================

1. Explore the entire accessible application.
2. Do not stop after the main dashboard.
3. Open every meaningful module and submenu.
4. Explore actual workflows, not just pages.
5. Do not click headings or decorative text unnecessarily.
6. Use meaningful user interactions.
7. Verify results using assertions/observations.
8. Do not invent features.
9. Do not invent expected behavior when requirements are unknown.
10. Do not duplicate test cases.
11. Separate independent business workflows.
12. Treat dynamic data as dynamic.
13. Do not bypass CAPTCHA.
14. Do not bypass authentication.
15. Do not attempt destructive security testing.
16. Do not expose credentials in the final report.
17. Prioritize payment and financial workflows.
18. Record actual UI labels and observed behavior.
19. Record validation messages exactly where useful.
20. Before finalizing, verify that all accessible modules have been explored.

MOST IMPORTANT:

First perform complete application exploration.

Then build the test plan from the observed application.

Do NOT generate Playwright automation code yet.

The immediate goal is:

COMPLETE APPLICATION EXPLORATION → COMPLETE QA TEST PLAN → DETAILED TEST CASES → E2E FLOWS → COVERAGE MATRIX
